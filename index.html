Local smug enjoying a 2012-2014 vintage

The children can make their own breakfast now. Their father is taking it extremely well.

By the Smug.ie desk5 September 2026 · 20:15 IST1 min read · Satire

A local smug with children of a 2012-2014 vintage has been spotted finishing an entire drink while it is still the temperature it was served at.

“They're at a great age,” he explained to a friend currently surviving on four hours' sleep. “I got a run in this morning. Might go for a swim tomorrow.” He then paused, apparently expecting this to be helpful.

The vintage refers to the children. After years of nappies, night feeds and cutting toast into the wrong shape, they have matured into people who can find the cereal themselves. Their father has matured into someone who tells you about it.

Dad completes 10k before anyone in the house needs anything

A local smug has described going for a run with only his keys as “a completely different stage of life”. Previous outings required a buggy, wipes, emergency raisins and a negotiated departure window.

Dad discovers the only person he has to dry and dress after a swim is himself

A dad has emerged from the sea visibly moved by the realisation that nobody needs to be wrestled into a vest in the car park.
